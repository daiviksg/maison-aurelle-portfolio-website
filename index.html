The concept

A quiet community of twelve houses, designed around privacy, wellbeing and a slower way of living by the water.

Contemporary architecture in warm, local materials, a landscape that is allowed to grow wild, and rooms arranged so that every one of them faces the lake.

Est. 2028

The quiet shore

Aurelle-sur-Lac

A village of three hundred people, one bakery and a ferry that still runs on a timetable. The lake is deep enough to stay cold in August and clear enough to see the stones at four metres.

Between ridge and water

Surrounded by walking trails, a sailing club and the old spa town across the water, the houses sit close to everything a slow life needs — and are designed not around arriving, but around returning.
